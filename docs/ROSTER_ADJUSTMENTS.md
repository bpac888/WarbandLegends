# Element and Faction Adjustments

- Mosswitch Brynna changes from Blue to Green. Her Common rarity, support role, stats, Special and artwork stay the same. Her rune charging, elemental interactions, matching-color feeding and sigil requirements now follow Green.
- Solar Scribe moves from Dune Riders to Nether. His new lore describes a keeper of dying sunlight and the names of departed souls. His Yellow element, Common rarity, attack role, stats, Special and artwork stay the same.

Common elements now total 5 Red, 5 Blue, 6 Green, 6 Yellow and 5 Purple. Stable character and copy IDs are preserved.
