# Void-Herald Mara — Nether Support

Mara moves from Dragon Dynasty to Nether and changes from ATK to SUP. She remains a Purple Common with Average speed (10 tiles), the same ID and the same artwork.

Her Special, **Graveward**, heals one chosen living ally for **28% of their maximum HP** and grants that ally **Barrier for one board move**. It does not heal the whole team or deal damage. Her Common support baselines are **850 HP, 400 ATK, 475 DEF**. Her new title and lore describe a Nether guide who shelters the living from the dead.

Other character records are unchanged. Existing owned copies and team slots resolve the same stable character ID. Historical fingerprint checks permit only this explicitly recorded change alongside prior approved revisions.
