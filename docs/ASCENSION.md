# Hero progression

Legends has four rarities. Each owned copy starts in ascension tier 1 at level 1. Feed unused Common heroes for at least 360 XP each, or Rare heroes for three times that XP (at least 1,080). Matching color grants 25% more XP. A Rare donor fed to another copy of the same character grants five times its normal Rare XP (15× the equivalent Common XP), including the color bonus and XP carried by a trained donor. Battles and some quests also grant XP. XP alone determines levels within the current Ascension or Limit Break cap. Feeding costs 25 gold per donor and permanently consumes that copy. Specials are always at full power; training, ascension and Limit Breaks do not change the Special coefficients, effects, mana speed or passive. Higher ATK can still raise the damage of an ATK-based Special.

## Ascension

| Rarity | Overall cap by tier | Final normal cap |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Common | 20 → 30 | 30 |
| Rare | 30 → 40 → 50 | 50 |
| Legendary | 40 → 50 → 60 → 70 | 70 |
| Mythical | 40 → 50 → 60 → 70 → 80 | 80 |

Reach the current overall cap, then spend the shown gold, Star Thread, matching-color Sigils and (for higher rarities) Aether Crystals or Sovereign Crowns. Ascension immediately adds 2% to ATK, DEF and HP, opens the next cap and displays the new tier at level 1. Earned XP, actual overall level, collection, Specials and combat power are retained. The tier notation is a display of progress within each tier: for example, a Legendary at overall level 40 becomes tier 2 level 1, then tier 2 level 11 at overall level 50. A battle already in progress retains its starting stats.

## Limit Breaks

After full ascension and the final normal level, Rare, Legendary and Mythical heroes can take two Limit Breaks. Commons have no Limit Breaks. Each break adds 3% immediate ATK, DEF and HP and opens five further overall levels. The hero still needs XP to reach those levels, from Common or Rare feeders, battles or quests. LB2 requires reaching the LB1 cap first.

| Rarity | Normal max | LB1 max | LB2 max |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Common | 30 | — | — |
| Rare | 50 | 55 | 60 |
| Legendary | 70 | 75 | 80 |
| Mythical | 80 | 85 | 90 |

LB1 costs gold, Awakened Embers and matching-color Sigils. LB2 costs more of those plus Astral Hearts. Nightmare dungeon victories drop Embers and Hearts; the Supply Exchange sells materials for earned gold or Gems. The dossier previews exact owned/required counts and the immediate stat result. At LB2, an Attack hero opens each battle with Attack Up, a Defense hero with Defense Up, and a Support hero with Mana Surge; each lasts two board moves. This automatic boon applies at battle start, not to an already active fight. No hero copy is consumed by ascension or Limit Breaking.

## Stats and saves

Unascended level 1 starts at about 55% of the hero's original maximum stats. At the final normal level, raw level growth reaches the original roster baseline. Ascension adds 2% per completed step; Limit Breaks add 3% per step and additional level growth. Bonuses add to the level-derived stats and round to integers. Collection shows current stats and tier level; Codex previews the fully ascended, LB2 maximum (or Common normal maximum). Existing v0.17 profiles migrate to schema 12, retaining heroes, XP, resources and ascensions. Legacy feeding limits are discarded. Previous Awakening progress becomes LB1 if the hero is eligible; LB2 must be earned under the new rules. Older supported backup profiles migrate on import. An in-progress battle using the retired third Awakening rank may need to be restarted under the new rules. All 135 hero IDs and original Specials are unchanged.
