# Roster Name Audit

All 174 display names are unique after case and punctuation normalization. Twenty-five existing names were revised to separate close spelling and sound clusters. New character names were checked against the full roster. Shared faction words such as Dragon or Frost remain descriptive titles; the characters bearing them have distinct full names.

Only display names and literal lore/passive-description references change. Character IDs, duplicate-copy IDs, portraits, factions, elements, rarity, role, speed, stats, Specials, passive mechanics, owned copies and saved progress remain unchanged. Old audit reports retain their historical names.
