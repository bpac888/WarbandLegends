# Common and Rare Revision

Six Commons become Rare; thirteen Rares become Common. No artwork, character names, factions or roles changed. Only these nineteen characters have revised gameplay records.

Common elements: **5 Red, 6 Blue, 5 Green, 6 Yellow, 5 Purple**. Total rarity counts: **27 Common, 62 Rare, 83 Legendary, 17 Mythical**. Summon odds are unchanged. Feeding, ascension costs, level caps and Limit Break eligibility follow the new rarities automatically. Existing saves use normal loading and the new caps; no separate progress migration is applied, as requested.

Only the four demoted characters with permitted element changes move colors: Succubus Lilth and Desert Assassin become Red; Nadia and the Hooded Thrall become Blue.

Historical expansion checks reverse only this approved, strictly verified revision before comparing their original fingerprints. Other records remain unchanged.
