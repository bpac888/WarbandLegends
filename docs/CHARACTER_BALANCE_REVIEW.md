# Warband Legends — Character Balance Review

Reviewed 2026-10-28. **All 200 characters**, every charge tier, all twelve factions and current growth rules. **Eleven audit-led additions are implemented. All previous 189 character records and portraits are preserved; changes to their kits remain recommendations.**

## Main Judgment

The stat hierarchy is healthy: Commons are clearly below Rares, Legendaries and Mythicals, including comparisons within each role. Ability budgets are less consistent. Several older Rares carry complete healing/buff packages while some newer Legendaries have minimal kits. Some slow and charging characters do not gain enough payoff for the wait. Both Astral Mythicals have now received targeted improvements. The eleven new characters fill coverage gaps; they do not replace the pending kit recommendations.

Speed and rarity are the primary axes. Compare first access, repeat cost, actual recipients, duration, burst, utility and personal survivability. A slow Mythical should offer a substantial payoff; a fast Rare can retain a specialized niche without becoming the better general pick. Do not flatten every character into a fast attacker.

Before this pass, Alkyron needed 12 tiles for 190% area damage and two-turn Burn. Rimebow was more efficient against one enemy, while Alkyron was stronger across three enemies. He now needs **10 tiles**, deals **210% to all enemies** and **Dispels their buffs before the attack**. Burn and his immunities stay. Three Astrals reduce the new matching requirement to 8 tiles.

Asterox originally needed 10 tiles for one-turn Taunt/Barrier and personal Ward. Ashwind needed only 8 for stronger personal protection. Asterox now needs **8 tiles**, with **two-turn Taunt, two-turn team Barrier and two-turn team Damage Ward**. Ashwind retains longer Taunt and extra personal Defense Up; Asterox has a distinct team-protection role. Three Astrals reduce Asterox’s matching requirement to 6.4. Veldtborn’s 30% and Astral’s 25% matching mana substantially alter repeat speed: a 10-tile requirement becomes about 7.69 or 8 matching tiles, respectively. Azhar falls from 6.5 to 5. Nether’s 15% is an opening gain only, not a recurring speed increase. Bloodcourt/Wildgrove sustain, Chymera’s extra ailment turn and Frost immunity can outweigh small stat bonuses in suitable encounters. Dune Riders +10% DEF is roughly 6% more direct-hit bulk at 750 starting DEF, not 10% damage reduction. Umbra’s three-member chance averages 0.3% of starting ATK/DEF/max HP per turn and is variable. Faction kits are not equally strong in every encounter. Review cross-faction encounters before changing the explicitly requested rules.

## Method and Limits

Each Special tier was cast through the real resolver against one, three and four neutral enemies (1,600 HP, 750 DEF). Allied HP was standardized at 1,600 with 90% missing so healing capacity could be compared. The caster uses normal-max baseline ATK and its actual passive. Faction bonuses, enemy passives, incoming attacks, pre-existing effects and Limit Break opening buffs are absent from those fixtures. Self buffs, enemy debuffs, Pierce and effect order are real. DoT and Regeneration capacities assume their full duration survives; shields can expire or be dispelled and do not stack. Utility is inspected from rules and scopes; it is not assigned an invented universal score.

These are controlled cast measurements and structural analysis, **not measured matchup win rates or proof that a character always wins**. They do not include board luck, enemy timing, overheal in live fights or every possible five-character composition. Counters, revival, immunities, Cleanse, Dispel and mana control are situational strengths. Recommendations need encounter tests before implementation.
