# Warband Legends Roster Audit

The expansion adds 15 champions: **1 Mythical, 11 Legendary and 3 Rare**, with **8 ATK, 4 DEF and 3 SUP**. All 135 existing character records and portraits are preserved. This audits roster coverage and kit structure; it does not claim equal combat strength or measured PvP win rates.

Every color has **30 champions**. ATK remains the majority: **77/150 (51.3%)**, versus 34 DEF and 39 SUP. Mythicals are **15/150 (10%)**; no further Mythicals are recommended in the next group. Rarity-wide average ATK, DEF and HP are unchanged.

Average is most common (72/150, 48%). Fast has 28, Slow 24, Charging 15, Very Fast 8 and Very Slow 3. Charging uses cumulative gates of 8/10 or 5/10/15. **Yshmir remains the only Very Slow Mythical.** The next additions should favor Fast and Charging with a few slower heavy hitters; keep Very Fast damage and utility restrained. No need to add more Very Slow champions merely to equalize counts.

## Remaining Gaps

Faction totals and overall colors are healthy. The useful gaps are combinations: Ironclad lacks purple, Chymera lacks red, Bloodcourt lacks blue and Umbra lacks red. Umbra (11/14 purple), Bloodcourt (9/14 red), Frost (9/14 blue) and Wildgrove (7/13 green) remain strongly color-focused. Preserve that identity while offering a few alternatives.

Legendary roles are 30 ATK, 15 DEF and 13 SUP. Future defender slots should be chosen for missing color/faction coverage rather than increasing every role equally. Accessible Rare healers and defenders remain useful alongside an ATK-majority expansion.

Summon odds stay **50% Common, 30% Rare, 7.5% Legendary, 1.5% Mythical and 11% twin-Common**. These are outcome odds, not roster shares. More Legendaries reduce the chance of a specific Legendary within its pool; a future targeted faction summon could help collection without increasing Mythical frequency.

## Validation and Limits

`npm run roster:audit` regenerates this report and its machine-readable cross tables. The build and expansion tests verify an exact SHA-256 match of all 135 original records. Combat tests exercise every new Special and charge tier, supported effects, summon eligibility, save compatibility and Auto actions. Browser checks cover artwork loading, character details and mobile layouts. Actual competitive balance still needs play data; coverage counts cannot prove matchup fairness.
