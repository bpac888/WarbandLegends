## Validation and Limits

Summon rarity odds and the ten-call guarantee are unchanged; new champions join their matching rarity pools. Build and audit checks fingerprint all 215 original records and source records; the build also verifies every original portrait hash. Historical checks retain their original bounded batches. New-character checks cover balanced coverage, unique names, Specials, summon eligibility, save compatibility, Auto actions and portrait quality. This is a coverage audit, not a measured matchup win-rate study. The [200-character audit](history/ROSTER-AUDIT-200.md) is archived. The [189-character audit](history/ROSTER-AUDIT-189-PRE-200.md) is archived. Previous reports remain available for [174](history/ROSTER-AUDIT-174.md), [162](history/ROSTER-AUDIT-162.md) and [150](history/ROSTER-AUDIT-150.md) champions.
