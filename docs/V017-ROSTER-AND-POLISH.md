# v0.17 · Roster, feeding and summoning

Legends remains an independent game. The original Warband Legends repository and its saves are untouched. All 135 Legends hero IDs and art paths remain stable.

Fourteen named former Commons are now Rares: Venn Sablethread; Tali, Scrapshot; Tovin Glassheart; Nima Dewthread; Redknife Tavin; Rimebow Tessa; Mavra Dusktrail; Kesh Duststep; Gallows Marksman Oren; Fjordwatch Hald; Edrik Mournwall; Nadia, the Patchwork Witch (`neon_glitch_weaver`); Succubus Lilth (`succubus_lilth`); and Boran, Chrome Fist (`neon_scrap_enforcer`). The latter three retain their established displayed spellings and all internal IDs. Their stats now follow the Rare reference tier. Rare support healing was strengthened, and Rare defensive Barrier Specials protect allies. The twenty remaining Commons comprise four red, four blue, four green, four yellow and four purple heroes. Six thematic color adjustments to existing Rares keep the full 135-hero roster exactly balanced at 27 per element.

Ashwind Warden, Frost Sage and Sable, Pale Matriarch are now Fast Mythicals (eight matching-color tiles). Their Special multipliers are recalculated for the faster cast pace.

A 100-Gem portal call has outcomes: Common 50%, Rare 30%, Legendary 7.5%, Mythical 1.5%, and twin Commons 11%. Twin Commons gives two independently owned hero copies; the same design can appear twice. Ten calls cost 900 Gems. If the first nine calls award only Commons, a Common or twin-Common final outcome becomes Rare. The portal shows both arrivals and identifies twin outcomes.

Gold alone no longer buys hero levels. Every consumed, unassigned Common unlocks three levels for one target hero, adds XP, and costs 25 Gold. Same-element donors give 25% more XP. Battles and quests can bank XP, but visible level and battle stats cannot exceed the feeding limit or current ascension cap. Team members, saved-team members and heroes in an active battle remain protected. Existing v0.16 saves migrate to the new schema with earned levels preserved; an individual copy's feeding limit is at least its earned level. Backups from v0.16 also import.

A first clear of each Campaign stage now pays 200 Gems; Dungeon first clears remain 100 Gems. Early summoning, first-victory, normal-stage milestone, ten-champion and first-ascension quests pay more Gems. This gives players additional calls to acquire Common donors. The visual pass updates feeding and summoning feedback, card and shop legibility, focus states, and battle labels without changing the existing art or battle layout.
