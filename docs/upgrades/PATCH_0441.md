# 0.44.1 — compact battle previews

Campaign, Dungeon and Arena confirmations show the Energy icon and cost inside Confirm, without a separate Energy row. Costs, disabled-entry rules and battle actions are unchanged.

On phones, campaign milestone previews use the available safe viewport height. The image expands into the saved space, with location-specific focal positions so important gates and sanctuaries remain visible in the taller framing. Desktop previews retain landscape art. The original full scene remains available in story panels. Loot Ticket controls and expandable faction rules remain accessible.

Validation: full unit suite/build; all 15 milestone previews on phone/desktop, image resolution/framing, visible Confirm and correct embedded Energy cost; expanded faction text contrast and existing story/reward flows.
