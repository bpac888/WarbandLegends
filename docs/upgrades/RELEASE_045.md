# 0.45.0 — story before campaign confirmation

Selecting stage 1, 5, 10, 15, 20 or 25 now opens its short story first on every difficulty. Continue records the viewed scene and opens the existing large-artwork/team confirmation. Only the final Confirm enters battle and spends Energy. Ordinary stages retain their direct preview. Closing either page spends nothing; retrying a battle retains its existing direct-retry behavior.

The story page removes its duplicate illustration and act heading from the body. A compact act label sits in the header beside the close button. Chapter title, objective and left/right dialogue use tighter spacing. Continue replaces Skip and Begin Battle. Journal reading remains available, and aftermath/reward panels keep their artwork.

Stage 1 now uses the same story → art confirmation → battle format, with the matching difficulty's world map as its introduction artwork. Stages 5–25 retain their distinct location art. No costs, rewards, difficulty rules or save keys change.

Verification: unit suite/build; all special-stage flows across phone/desktop; stage 1 first and repeated entries; no Energy spent until Confirm; legacy viewed markers, Journal replays and chapter rewards; contrast and Loot Ticket flow.
