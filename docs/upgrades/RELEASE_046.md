# 0.46.0 — Campaign story rewrite

The campaign now tells one continuing story across the three difficulty maps:

- **Normal — The Stolen Light:** Yshmir promises to restore the kingdom while manipulating Auroline into weakening his prison. The party stops her engine, but its stored charge escapes and starts the fires.
- **Hard — The Last Road Out:** The party opens an evacuation route. Yshmir corrupts the attempted containment; breaking the Crown's outer ring saves the refugees but collapses the citadel.
- **Nightmare — The Last Command:** The collapse exposes the inner lock. The party escorts Auroline through the ruins, resists Yshmir's false promises, and closes the prison. The survivors return to rebuild.

Nima, the Keeper, Auroline and Yshmir are the recurring speakers. Scenes identify the actual milestone opponents, including Auroline at Normal 25 and Brukk's wardens at Nightmare 25. Repeated opponents retreat or regroup rather than being killed and inexplicably returning.

Existing milestone paintings remain appropriate to the crossings, gates, gardens and Crown. No artwork or video changes were needed. Story-first entry, the large artwork confirmation, journal entries, existing save keys and chapter rewards are preserved.

Validation: unit suite and production build; mobile/desktop campaign scene, journal and entry checks; contrast checks. Guardian IDs are checked against the encounter formations.
