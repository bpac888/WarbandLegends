# 0.47.0 — Campaign, weekly challenges and compact presentation

The campaign follows Auroline's kidnapping, the evacuation of the burning valley, and the return to the ruined citadel to bind Yshmir. Runa replaces Nima as the companion. The player speaks under their profile name; the default is Commander. The ending now has Quill poisoning the roots at Nightmare 20 and Yshmir fighting beside the controlled Brukk and Tessa at Nightmare 25. Both encounters use the opponents described in the scenes.

Editorial revision removed the engine/charge/outer-ring explanations, clarified why each disaster happens, varied the chapter goals, and resolved Brukk's relationship with Runa. Full reviewed dialogue is in [CAMPAIGN_STORY.md](../CAMPAIGN_STORY.md). New ending illustrations show the poisoned gardens and root-supported prison. Other milestone paintings still fit the locations.

## Weekly challenges

Each challenge contains three separately entered battles. The boss and its mechanic appear in battle 3 with guards or mechanic units. Wins save progress; failure retries the current battle. Each battle starts with restored HP/mana. No Energy cost; one gift after all three wins. Monday 00:00 Pacific reset, five-week cycle anchored November 9, 2026. Completed old gifts remain completed, preventing duplicate rewards.

| Week | Trial | Boss |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Runesmith: Blast in battle 1, Prismatic in battle 2, both in battle 3; forge and activate manually | Mirror Mage copies the latest player Special, including its selected charge level, on its next attack |
| 2 | Five Rares only | Twin Tyrants: defeat both before the enemy phase; otherwise one revives at 40% HP |
| 3 | Borrowed Warband: fixed Lv. 40 loaned team; six curated combinations rotate on successive returns | Mana Collector stores stolen mana in two crystals; destruction returns it, every third move consumes it to heal |
| 4 | Final Volley: three distinct fixed boards, loaned team, charged Specials; three moves per battle | Eclipse Beast alternates 75% tile/Special resistance every two moves |
| 5 | Twofold: use only the featured pair and include both; all ten pairs rotate before repeating | Venom Regent starts with 20%-HP Barrier (3 moves) and full charge; first action after move 1 grants ATK Up/DEF Up and team Poison for two turns |

Trials award 100 Gems + 500 Gold; bosses award 150 Gems + 750 Gold. Loaned teams never change owned champions or saved teams. Cards show the active team/pair and three-battle progress. Ten dedicated challenge paintings and a mana-crystal portrait are local assets. The abandoned hourglass painting and retired campaign paintings rotate only while battle portraits are actually loading; no artificial wait or extra startup screen.

## Presentation and names

- Compact weekly cards combine progress/rewards and show the claim control only after completion.
- Story dialogs fit their content, with readable scene titles, borderless close controls, alternating left/right turns, plain speaker names and speech tails.
- Battle details, charge selection and active-effects panels use muted parchment, dark text and crimson controls. Repeated summaries, lore and generic explanations were removed from battle inspection.
- Short Guide rules use icons and familiar abbreviations.
- Shorter board cells leave space for readable status art over the lower portraits. Frames remain fixed when effects appear/expire; portrait movement/zoom is replaced with glow feedback. The enemy targeting arc is removed.
- Startup uses the intro's first frame instead of the rune loading message.
- Liora Dewthread replaces Nima's display/lore name, retaining the internal ID, art, abilities and progress. Historical roster checks explicitly permit only that audited identity change.
