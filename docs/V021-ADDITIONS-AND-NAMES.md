# Twelve New Champions and Distinct Roster Names

Added the approved twelve champions: 9 Legendary and 3 Rare; 8 ATK, 2 DEF and 2 SUP. The roster now has 174 characters. All twelve have original 768×1152 portraits in the established detailed painterly fantasy style, faction-specific lore, named Specials and supported mana speeds. Pyrrhon and Thyssara have two charge tiers; Kozhak has three.

The [current roster audit](ROSTER_AUDIT.md) lists the additions and faction, element, rarity, role and speed counts. The [name audit](NAME_AUDIT.md) lists 25 revised names, including Sahir → Yaqut, Velka → Cressida and Lyra → Octavia. Names and their literal lore/passive-description references change; existing IDs, portraits, stats, factions, affinities, kits, passive mechanics and progress remain unchanged.

Preservation tests reverse only the audited text edits, then verify the complete historical records against their original fingerprints. Additional checks reject unapproved identity edits and verify that renamed heroes retain copy IDs, teams, XP and summon history. Roster regeneration retains the current complete records.

Rarity-wide average maximum HP, ATK and DEF are unchanged. Summon odds, the ten-call guarantee, feeding rules, energy and progression are unchanged. Mythicals remain at 15.

Validation: all 26 native test files pass, including every new Special tier, effect recipient, summoning/save round trips and six completed Auto encounters across three new team compositions. Browser validation covers all 174 Codex portraits, twelve new character pages across four viewport widths and 25 renamed character pages at phone and desktop sizes.
