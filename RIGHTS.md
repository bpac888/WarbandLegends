# Rights and provenance

This separate game was requested by the owner of `bpac888/warband-legends`. It reuses that owner's champion definitions, lore, kits, passive definitions and artwork under their direction. No general public reuse license is granted by this repository. The owner should review rights before changing repository visibility or granting a license.

Source snapshot: `bpac888/warband-legends` at `acf554c4666208758a0c198f9fc2c1aef6e54aa4`. Source champion definitions: `backend/server.py`, `backend/veldtborn_data.py`, `backend/roster_expansion_data.py`; ability/passive definitions: `backend/effects_data.py`. Each imported champion records its original template ID and source image path in `data/champions.json`. IDs with original spaces or capitalization remain unchanged.

All 135 portraits are bundled locally as optimized WebP copies of the owner's existing art. Scene backgrounds derive from the owner's fight-colosseum and summoning-portal artwork. No runtime request to the original game is made. The importer does not connect to its database.

The 15-champion expansion adds original image-generated portraits, lore and match-3 kits created for Warband Legends. These are separate 768×1152 WebP files, with no third-party character artwork extracted. All 135 earlier champion records and portraits are retained unchanged. `data/roster-expansion.json` records the preserved catalog fingerprints and new IDs.

The rune symbols, interface, match-3 engine, tests and standalone tooling were implemented for this repository. The supplied Empires & Puzzles screenshot and gameplay notes informed the requested genre mechanics; no screenshot art, characters, brand, proprietary assets or game code from that game are included.

The v0.2 Legends roster independently reassigns affinities, rescales stats, and combines original A2/A3 effects into one Special. The original imported records are archived in `data/source-champions.json` and `data/source-effects.json` for provenance and regeneration; those archives are excluded from the runtime build. These changes apply only to Legends.

The v0.3 vector rune artwork was replaced in v0.5 by fifteen original image-generated shield, dragon-crest and prismatic-gem assets. The user’s reference informed color and shape readability; its pixels were not extracted. The generated sheet was separated into equal cells and encoded as local WebP artwork inside SVG wrappers, sized for high-density display at actual board tile sizes. The files have a 320-unit viewBox and contain raster art; they are not infinitely detailed vectors. `tools/make-runes.mjs` checks these curated assets. The new five-element crest/favicon is original vector artwork. Portal motion adapts the owner’s Warband Legends activation concept into this game without modifying the source game. Status icons, the effect guide, jewel/bronze styling and feedback were implemented for Legends; no third-party game UI assets are included.

The v0.6 world-map background is original image-generated fantasy scenery created for Legends. Its local WebP data is embedded in `assets/scenes/world-map.svg`. Mode portraits reuse the owner’s existing art. Campaign markers, card symbols, lane guides and elemental animations were implemented for this game.

The v0.8 dungeon illustrations are optimized copies of the owner's seven `frontend/src/assets/dungeons/*.png` images; the Wyrm portrait comes from `backend/static/bosses/magma_dragon.png` at the same source snapshot. These local SVG wrappers embed WebP copies without changing the compositions. The Hard and Nightmare campaign maps were generated as edited versions of Legends' original v0.6 world map to retain its geography and progressively show destruction. No original-game files were modified. Campaign stage count, difficulty names and most later stage names adapt `backend/battle_modes.py`; fights, progression, scaling and rewards are independently implemented for Legends.

The v0.9 Citadel destinations reuse `frontend/src/assets/menu/{summoning-portal.png,fight-colosseum.png,codex-factions-pavilion.png,forge-armory-hd.png}`. Arena uses `roman-arena.png`; Special Events use the three original `frontend/src/assets/events/*.webp` illustrations. These are optimized local copies. Summoning adapts the owner's `frontend/src/pages/Summon.jsx` and `frontend/src/components/SummonPortal.jsx`/`.css` into the independent vanilla-JavaScript UI. Arena preset identities/formations and event calendars/bosses adapt `backend/battle_modes.py` and `backend/events.py` at the recorded source snapshot. No original files were changed.

The v0.16 shop materials are twelve new, image-generated fantasy inventory illustrations: ten ascension/awakening resources plus a gold pouch and Gem chest. They were composed as an original 4×3 atlas and exported into local 300-pixel WebP assets. The older SVG material icons remain in the source archive; the live shop uses the new images. No art from the supplied third-party game reference was copied.

The audit-following expansion adds twelve more original image-generated portraits and new character designs, lore and kits. These use the same detailed painterly fantasy direction as the first expansion and are bundled as separate high-resolution WebP portraits. All prior 150 records and portraits remain unchanged; `data/roster-expansion-2.json` fingerprints that baseline and records the twelve implemented audit recommendations. The 150-champion audit is retained in `docs/history/ROSTER-AUDIT-150.md`.

The third expansion adds twelve original character portraits and faction-specific kits and lore in the established painterly fantasy style. The existing 162 portraits and gameplay records remain unchanged. Twenty-five display names and literal descriptive references are revised under the owner’s direction; unchanged IDs retain their original provenance. `data/roster-expansion-3.json` and `data/roster-name-audit.json` record the additions and text-only identity edits.

The Astral Concord expansion adds fifteen original image-generated portraits and one original faction emblem. Its alien designs loosely evoke public-domain mythical archetypes, with original names, lore and kits. No third-party character art is copied. All 174 prior characters are unchanged; `data/roster-expansion-4.json` fingerprints that baseline.

Artwork-quality restoration re-encodes 131 existing original-source portraits at native resolution and WebP quality 92. Their compositions and provenance are unchanged; no replacement artwork is generated. See `docs/ARTWORK_QUALITY.md`.

Eleven Legendary portraits receive image-generated edits explicitly referencing the current owner-provided in-game portraits, preserving their general designs while increasing detail and rendering quality. No third-party character art is used. Four separately demoted Rare characters retain their exact portrait files. See `docs/LEGENDARY_REFINEMENTS.md`.
